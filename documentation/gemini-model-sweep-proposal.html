Finish the execution and reporting foundation, then connect one Flash model to the benchmark workflow we already have.

What I heard

You want Steps 56 and 57 completed unattended, followed by useful progress toward a simple Gemini model. The first integration should produce inspectable answers without expanding the coding-agent benchmark or changing the calibration roster.

Why this shape

Step 56 makes provider selection explicit. Step 57 exposes execution and model identity in reports. Gemini then joins the same runner, scoring, budget, resume, and inspection paths through a small HTTP adapter.
