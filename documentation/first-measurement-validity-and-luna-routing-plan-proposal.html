Why this shape

The plan adds only the gates that the first observation proved were missing. It reuses the already-planned calibration and coding-agent work instead of forking new evaluators or scoring contracts.

Two independent rails, one coordinator.
The step numbers are append-only IDs—not permission to execute the file as a numeric phase. Every cross-plan transition is explicit and machine-verified.

Parallelism is narrow: Steps 56 and 62 may start together. Wait steps and cross-plan gates remain hard stops.

Not in v1

These exclusions keep a validity repair from becoming a new benchmark program or an operations product.

  • No Luna, Haiku, or other additions to DEFAULT_ROSTER; no Instrument A Luna adapter.
  • No changes to tier-judging-v0, Suite, RunRow, the official parser, conflict rule, or parse-failure zero.
  • No edits, renumbering, or duplication of pending canonical Steps 13–24 or 27–55.
  • No Steps 18–24 operations surfaces and no publication of the first run through them.
  • No row deletion salvage, first-label score, score pooling, or ledger claim from a single/unsealed run.
  • No service, scheduler, dashboard, or interactive mid-run approval workflow.

Risks and halt conditions

A red result is evidence. The plan preserves artifacts and stops at the owning step instead of falling back, tuning after looking, or asking for approval mid-run.

Claude CLI drift

Unsupported sealing flags, auth failure, a changed JSON envelope, or unresolved identity halt Step 56 before an unsealed call.

Operator-started local endpoint

Steps 13 and 59 require the explicit local OpenAI-compatible endpoint. An unresolved profile or unreachable endpoint aborts before any call; the plan never auto-starts or silently replaces it.

Metric instability

A below-control arm, changed pair direction, or >15-point range blocks Step 16. The fixed three runs stay preserved; there is no favorable fourth.

Containment nondeterminism

Anything below 8/8 returns to Step 62. Native Windows tests, selected skips, live identities, and retained-FD escapes cannot qualify Step 27.

Dirty-worktree isolation

Build worktrees will not inherit the current user-owned process/test and report/CLI edits. Those changes must be checkpointed by ownership before execution.

Historical contract mixing

Legacy data remains inspectable, but sealed rows cannot append to an unsealed run and requested aliases cannot stand in for provider identity.

Cross-plan bypass

An absent or stale receipt blocks the next plan. Numeric --resume never substitutes for an explicit plan, step, and verify-only check.
