02 · Headline

    
Suite score per model

    
Score is the suite mean × 100. The marker on each gauge is the
      degenerate floor — what a model scores by ignoring the question and always
      answering the single most common expected label. A score at the floor carries no evidence of
      reasoning.

  

  






  

    
03 · Anatomy

    
Three different kinds of zero

    
A zero-scored cell can mean three unrelated things. Only the first is a judging
      failure; collapsing them into one number hides that.

  

  

  






  

    
04 · Diagnostic

    
How much of the gap is formatting rather than judging?

  

  

    
This is a diagnostic, not a score. It re-reads the same stored
      responses under a deliberately different rule — first recognizable label wins — purely
      to size how much of each model’s zero-scoring is answer formatting rather than judgment.
      Choosing a parser after seeing the data is how a benchmark flatters itself, so this number is
      reported beside the real score and must never be quoted in its place.
